# Academy theme rules

- Use `cover` for formal title pages, `section` for major transitions, and `end` only for the final courtesy close.
- Treat each slide as one claim. Prefer short structured fragments to prose paragraphs.
- Start at normal density; move to compact or dense only after render-based overflow inspection.
- Use `split` for genuinely paired content. Wide figures should span the canvas in a content layout.
- Preserve the Academy blue visual hierarchy, generous alignment rhythm, readable typography, and 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Put verified citations or figure credits on the slide where evidence appears; never invent sources.
- Keep a substantive conclusions slide before the `end` slide, and give `end` an H1 heading.
- Render, inspect, and correct overflow, sparse composition, broken assets, and over-boxing before completion.
- Use the merged `academic-figure`, `scholarly-result`, `frankfurt-agenda`, and `cobalt-cards` layouts for their named content shapes while preserving Academy typography and spacing.

The theme-specific Skill contains layout/component examples and the complete validation rubric. Workspace safety, MCP usage, Harness invocation, and export workflow remain the responsibility of the common FastPPT Skill.
